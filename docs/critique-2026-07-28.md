## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored in composition and motion; rented in the token layer. Roughly 60/40 against.

Authored: the hero threads the portrait between MAC (z-1) and HARNESS (z-3); the projects table dims
every OTHER row title on hover, which is the authored inversion; the masonry packs against build-time
intrinsic dimensions so 61 images are correct on first paint.

Rented: Space Grotesk + Space Mono + Caslon on near-black is the default premium-creative stack, and
font-sans and font-display are the same font so the display token is dead weight. #c9aa6e is a category
gold, not Mac's. The glass utility is 2021 glassmorphism applied over photographs. rounded-2xl and
RADIUS=14 make every frame read as a UI card rather than a print.

Authorship inversion: Gallery.svelte forces 8 photographs into auto-rows-[128px] with hardcoded SPANS
and object-cover, so a CSS array recomposes the frames on the surface that proves Mac has an eye.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs exit 2, three findings, one actionable (overused-font, app.html:9, Space
Grotesk). Two broken-image findings are false positives: one matched inside a script doc comment in
Pic.svelte, the other matched the literal text of an img tag inside a JS comment in gallery/+page.ts.

Visual overlays: unavailable. The browser pane does not composite, so injection would have produced
nothing a human could see. Computed-style and network measurement used instead.

## Overall Impression

The engineering is better than the design, and the design is better than the content. There is genuine
craft here (three failsafes on motion arming, documented filter and will-change tradeoffs), but the site
sells a business that does not match the work on it, and it announces 54 photographs to screen readers as
camera serial numbers.

Biggest opportunity: decide what Mac shoots. Every word says portraits and weddings; all three projects
are coastal editorial, automotive, and motorsport.

## What's Working

1. ProjectsTable prewarm: requestIdleCallback plus img.decode(), deferred off first paint, deliberately
   skipping the placehold.co fallback so page load never reaches a third party.
2. The motion-arming system: pre-paint inline script, targeted CSS, disarm on GSAP handoff, and three
   independent failsafes so content can never be stranded invisible.
3. The masonry's documented tradeoffs: build-time intrinsics, WAAPI instead of GSAP, EAGER_COUNT=12,
   opacity instead of filter with the Firefox stall explained in a comment.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Every italic is a browser-synthesised fake

font-serif is Libre Caslon Display, which ships no italic at all (verified: ital@1 returns HTTP 400,
while Libre Caslon Text returns 200). Seven files rely on it, including "worth keeping" on the contact
headline at up to 128px.

Fix: switch to Libre Caslon Text with an ital axis and keep Display for the hero wordmark only via a
separate token; or drop every italic class and let text-gold carry the emphasis it already carries.
Command: /impeccable typeset

### [P0] 54 of 61 gallery images are announced as camera filenames

Measured in the rendered DOM: alt="_DSC0373", alt="D7324228-2C59-47CB-8FAD-0959AB77B8B0". Root cause is
Pic.svelte:25 falling back to pic.name when the caption is empty, and all 61 metadata files ship an empty
caption. Ph.svelte separately leaks alt="HERO PORTRAIT" and alt="MAC · PORTRAIT".

Fix: give Ph.svelte an explicit alt prop, change Pic.svelte's fallback to an empty string, then write the
captions. Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] /gallery ships 7.5 MB because the responsive ladder has no small rungs

7,558 KB transferred, identical at 375x812 and desktop. Each picture element emits only two candidates;
for _DSC0373 and _DSC0374 the srcset is 3000w and 6000w against a 230 CSS px slot, so the smallest option
is 13x too large. static/project-media (7 files, 3.4 MB) bypasses enhanced-img entirely.

Fix: widen the enhanced-img width ladder so the floor matches the real slot size, and route project-media
through the pipeline or resize the sources. Command: /impeccable optimize

### [P1] The homepage gallery is a dead end with a false affordance, eight times

Gallery.svelte renders a figure with group-hover:scale-105 and no anchor, and the section has no link to
/gallery at all. Hover-scale is a universal clickable signal.

Fix: wrap each figure in an anchor to /gallery, add a "View all 61 frames" link beside the date range, and
rename the nav item from Work to Gallery. Command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] Placeholder identity is concentrated at the conversion point

Contact links to instagram.com and behance.net site roots, "Journal" points at #top, and the phone number
is bare text with no tel: link. About claims a Lisbon studio and portrait and wedding work; all three
projects are Florida coast, automotive, and motorsport, each with the word placeholder in its own intro.

Fix: real profile URLs or delete those rows, then decide the positioning and make copy and work agree.
Command: /impeccable harden

### [P2] Measured accessibility gaps across every page

- Contrast: color-ink-faint #6f6c66 on #070708 is 3.85:1, failing 4.5:1, and it is used only at 10.5 to
  13px so the large-text allowance never applies. 26 failing nodes. The other ink tokens pass at 17.8:1,
  8.0:1 and 9.1:1.
- Focus: 4 of 21 interactive elements on the homepage carry an authored focus-visible style; 1 of 12 on
  /projects. Nav links, footer links and the mailto have none.
- Tap targets: nav links are 16px tall and the mobile hamburger is 40x40. Nothing meets 44x44.
- Two h1 elements on the homepage (MAC and HARNESS), one logical title split in two.
- Reduced-motion is honoured in app.html, motion.ts and app.css but ignored by MasonryGallery's FLIP and
  its smooth scrollTo.

Command: /impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags

Jordan (first-timer): homepage photographs scale on hover but do nothing on click, eight times; /gallery is
only reachable via a nav item labelled Work, which he already assigned to the section he scrolled past; he
cannot tell whether Mac shoots his thing; Project.svelte offers two identical ghost buttons.

Casey (distracted mobile): the hero's max-[760px]:hidden deletes the only positioning sentence on the site,
so her first screen is two gold words and a Book a session pill with no explanation; /projects instructs her
to hover a row when the preview is hidden at media(hover:none); break-all fractures the 44px serif email at
375px; she downloads the full 7.5 MB gallery.

Sam (screen reader and reduced-motion): /gallery is 61 buttons named Expand _DSC0373; role=list over bare
anchors is invalid ARIA so rows are dropped or miscounted; one click reflows 61 tiles and steals the
viewport regardless of reduced-motion; +error.svelte can clip its own recovery links with scrolling disabled.

## Minor Observations

- Hero.svelte:93 styles .t-film, an element that no longer exists, and the comment above it describes a
  composition that is gone.
- The hero runs three hardcoded layout regimes with magic percentages; any copy change breaks the overlap.
- Fonts load via a render-blocking third-party stylesheet with no preload, so the 232px wordmark FOUTs
  through Space Grotesk on first visit.
- About.svelte wraps a clause in text-ink inside a parent that is already text-ink, a no-op.
- Two placehold.co runtime fallbacks on an otherwise self-contained prerendered site.
- Gallery.svelte says 2018 to 2026; /gallery says 2022 to 2026.
- src/lib/content/pictures/ is 184 MB, of which 139 MB is six committed NEF raws at roughly 23 MB each, all
  tracked, with no gitignore rule.
- overflow-x: hidden on html and body is a symptom of the hero's absolute positioning and will break any
  future position: sticky.

## Questions to Consider

1. The work is cars, a pit lane and a coastline, and every word says portraits and weddings. Which is the
   business? If it is motorsport, the champagne-on-black Caslon palette is bridal-adjacent and wrong.
2. What is the reward for clicking a photograph? Currently a slightly larger tile with no caption, no full
   resolution, no next or previous, and no exit but clicking again.
3. Does the overlapping wordmark survive being alone, as it is on mobile with the blurb hidden?
4. Why is the loudest thing on the contact screen a sentence and the quietest the action?
5. At handover, what does the new owner inherit? The developer's name as the only gold word in the footer,
   four CDN fonts, two placehold.co fallbacks, a fake phone number, 139 MB of raws, and three projects whose
   own intros say placeholder.
