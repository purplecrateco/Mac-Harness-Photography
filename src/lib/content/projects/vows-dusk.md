## Last light over the valley

**Vows at Dusk** follows a single evening from the quiet of the getting-ready
rooms to the first dance under string lights. The brief was simple: stay invisible,
keep it warm, let the light do the work.

The ceremony landed exactly at golden hour — the kind of light you can't fake and
can barely plan for.

### Coverage

1. Pre-ceremony, both suites
2. The walk and the vows
3. Portraits in the olive grove
4. Reception, candid through to the dance floor

> The best frames came in the ten minutes after everyone assumed the photos were done.

Delivered as a 90-image story plus a tightly edited 18-frame highlight set.
