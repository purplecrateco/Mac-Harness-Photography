## A coast built for the last hour of light

**Golden Hour, Gulf Side** is a small placeholder set from the sugar-sand
panhandle. Flat wide beaches, a pier that seems to run out forever, and about
fifteen minutes where the whole town goes pastel.

Most of the series comes back to the pier. Long lines, cool water, warm sky:

![The pier at dusk, running out into a calm Gulf](/project-media/coast-pier.jpg)

> The light here doesn't drop so much as dissolve.

A few blocks inland the water tower picks up the same flat evening light. You
can tell exactly where you are without being told.

![Navarre Beach water tower over the sound](/project-media/coast-watertower.jpg)

### What's in the set

- 18 frames across three evenings
- Mixed wide landscapes and a few loose portraits
- Edited warm, shadows left deep on purpose

One portrait to close it out, shot straight into the setting sun:

![Backlit portrait on the shore at sunset](/project-media/coast-portrait.jpg)

That last frame is the one that sold the edit for me. I'd shoot the other three
evenings again just to get back to it.
