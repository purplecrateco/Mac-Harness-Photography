## A coast built for the last hour of light

**Golden Hour, Gulf Side** is a small placeholder set shot along the sugar-sand
panhandle — wide flat beaches, a pier that runs out forever, and a sky that
turns the whole town pastel right before dark.

The pier is the anchor of the whole series. Long lines, cool water, warm sky:

![The pier at dusk, running out into a calm Gulf](/project-media/coast-pier.jpg)

> The light here doesn't drop. It dissolves.

Inland a few blocks, the town water tower catches the same flat evening light —
the kind of quiet landmark that says exactly where you are without a caption.

![Navarre Beach water tower over the sound](/project-media/coast-watertower.jpg)

### What's in the set

- 18 frames across three evenings
- Mixed wide landscapes and a few loose portraits
- Edited warm, shadows left deep on purpose

A single portrait to close it out, shot straight into the setting sun:

![Backlit portrait on the shore at sunset](/project-media/coast-portrait.jpg)

That last frame is the one that sold the whole edit — everything else is just
setting the stage for the moment the sun finally lets go.
