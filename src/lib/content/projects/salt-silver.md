## On the shoreline

Shot over four cold mornings on a stretch of working coastline, **Salt & Silver**
chases the particular quiet of a place built for summer and abandoned to winter.
Everything here is film — no digital safety net, no second chances on the frame.

> The grain isn't a filter. It's the weather.

The series leans on a restrained palette: bleached timber, oxidised metal, and the
single warm note of low sun catching wet rope.

### What's in the set

- 24 selected frames from ~11 rolls
- Hand-scanned, dust left in on purpose
- Printed on uncoated cotton stock

A short note on process: each roll was push-processed one stop to hold the shadows,
then scanned flat so the contrast lives in the print rather than the file.
