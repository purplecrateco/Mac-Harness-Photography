## The race nobody films

**Pit Lane, Pink** ignores the track and points the lens at the pit box, where
the actual race is run — fuel, tires, and a crew moving faster than the car ever
does on the straight.

The standout subject is a pink Porsche GT3 rolling into its box:

![Pink Porsche GT3 entering the pit box under the lights](/project-media/porsche-pit.jpg)

> On track it's a blur. In the lane, you can read every decal.

### Coverage

- Pit entry and stop sequences
- Crew over the wall, tight on hands and tools
- Sponsor branding and livery detail

A natural companion set would head skyward for the support paddock — same event,
totally different energy:

![Paramotor drifting over a cloud-streaked sky](/project-media/sky-paramotor.jpg)

Placeholder for now, but the pit-lane angle is the one worth building out into a
full endurance-weekend story.
