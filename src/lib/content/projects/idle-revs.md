## Cars where they actually live

**Idle Revs** is a rolling-by approach to car photography: no studio, no closed
course, just a low angle on a public road and a shutter slow enough to smear the
asphalt while the body stays sharp.

The hero frame is a blacked-out Supra at a clean panning speed:

![Black Toyota Supra panned against a storefront](/project-media/supra-street.jpg)

> Keep the car sharp, let everything else blur. That's the whole trick.

### Notes on the technique

1. 1/100s or slower, tracking the car through the frame
2. Single low point of focus on the front wheel arch
3. Shoot dozens — keepers are maybe one in twenty

This is intentionally a thin placeholder project, but the same panning setup
scales to a full series across a season of meets and street pulls.
