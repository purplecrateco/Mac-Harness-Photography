## Cars where they actually live

**Idle Revs** shoots cars rolling past on a public road rather than in a studio
or on a closed course. Low angle, and a shutter slow enough to smear the asphalt
while the body of the car stays sharp.

The hero frame is a blacked-out Supra at a clean panning speed:

![Black Toyota Supra panned against a storefront](/project-media/supra-street.jpg)

> Keep the car sharp and let everything else blur. That's most of the trick.

### Notes on the technique

1. 1/100s or slower, tracking the car through the frame
2. Single low point of focus on the front wheel arch
3. Shoot dozens, because keepers are maybe one in twenty

It's a thin placeholder for now, but the same panning setup would carry a full
series across a season of meets and street pulls.
